LLMTray Support

IPSupport LLC · for Mac, iPhone and iPad

Get help

  • In the Mac app: Settings › General › Report a Bug… prepares a report with LLMTray's logs and opens it in your mail app, attached to the email (or shown in Finder for you to attach): you can open and read it, and nothing is sent until you send it.
  • Email: bugreport@ipsupport.us, from any device. For the iPhone and iPad app, tell us the device model and what you asked; a screenshot helps.
  • GitHub: issues for bugs and ideas.

Requirements

Mac: macOS 14 or later on a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later). Models need free memory and disk space: the setup assistant shows each one's size.

iPhone and iPad: iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later. Models run on the device: the smaller ones need 6 GB of memory, the larger ones and making pictures 8 GB, the largest 12 GB. The app only offers models that fit your device and shows each one's download size.

Common questions

How do I …? The LLMTray guide covers models, chat, projects and files, tools, images, music, voice and connecting your apps. The same guide is in the app: ask about LLMTray in the Getting Started project.

Where are my models? On the Mac, in the folder chosen in Settings › Models. You can point LLMTray at a folder of MLX models you already have; the Mac App Store version asks you once to allow access to it.

How do other apps use LLMTray? On the Mac, start the server, then use the address shown in Settings › Server in any app that speaks the OpenAI API.

Coming from the Mac version downloaded from our site? In the Mac App Store version, Settings › General › Import from LLMTray (direct download) brings over your chats, projects, profiles and downloaded models, with no second copy of the models on disk.

How do I turn off usage statistics? On the Mac, Settings › General › Usage statistics; on iPhone and iPad, Settings › Usage Statistics. See the privacy policy for what is sent.

iPhone and iPad

How do I get a model? Tap the model name at the top of a chat and choose one. It downloads straight to your device, and then runs without a network connection. A download can be paused and resumed.

The model is slow or the app closes. Larger models and pictures need most of the device's memory: close other apps, and pick a smaller model if it keeps happening. Long work warms the device up, which slows it down.

How do I talk with it? Tap the waveform button next to the message field. Your speech is recognized on the device; pick your languages in Settings › Voice Conversation › Languages. Answers are read by the best voice installed on your device: better ones are in iOS Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices.

Calendar, reminders, maps and pictures are off until you turn them on in Settings; iOS then asks for access. Changing or deleting an event asks you first.

How do I delete my data? Swipe a chat to delete it, or a model in the model list. Deleting the app removes its chats and the models it downloaded; a model folder you opened from Files stays where it is.
