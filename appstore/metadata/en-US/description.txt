LLMTray runs AI models on your Mac. Chat, draw pictures, make songs and give your apps an OpenAI-compatible API, all on Apple silicon, with no account and no cloud.

LESS DATA CENTER. MORE MAC.
Your Mac is already a capable AI machine. LLMTray puts it to work: the models run right here, so your prompts, files and results never leave it, and nothing waits in a server queue.

CHAT WITH OPEN MODELS
• Pick a model that fits your Mac: a guided setup suggests the right size for your memory.
• Download more MLX models from Hugging Face in Settings.
• Models that see read your images; models that reason show their thought process.
• Tabs, history, search, temporary chats and automatic compaction of long chats.

YOUR FILES, WITH SOURCES
• Projects group chats with their own instructions and documents.
• Drop PDFs, Word, Markdown and text files into a project: the model searches them and cites the pages it used.
• Pin a short reference so every answer in the project sees it.

IMAGES AND SONGS, MADE ON YOUR MAC
• Ask for a picture in the chat: it is drawn right on your Mac.
• Edit an image by attaching it and saying what to change.
• Make a song with vocals and lyrics, or an instrumental, from a short description.
• Creator mode lets you adjust the prompt, size and model before anything is made.

TOOLS THAT STAY UNDER YOUR CONTROL
• Built-in tools for date and time, a calculator, and, only if you turn them on, web search, news, Wikipedia, weather and more.
• Let a chat tidy a folder you allow: it proposes new folders, moves and renames, and nothing changes until you approve. Undo takes it back.

CONNECT YOUR CODING AGENTS AND APPS
• LLMTray serves an OpenAI-compatible API on your Mac (localhost). Point any app or coding agent that speaks the OpenAI API at it.
• Each model gets a short alias; apps can switch models by name.
• Network access for other devices is off unless you turn it on.

MADE FAST
• Per-model profiles for sampling, KV cache quantization and speculative decoding.
• A built-in benchmark measures your Mac and can tune the server for it.

NOTHING YOU DIDN'T ASK FOR
• Every heavy feature is off until you turn it on, and nothing is downloaded before you choose it.
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Anonymous usage statistics are optional and can be turned off in Settings.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). Models need free memory and disk space: the setup shows each one's size before you download it.
